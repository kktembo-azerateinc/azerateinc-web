Privacy Policy

What happens to your information when you visit azerateinc.com, book a call, or email us. Short version: very little, and we don’t sell it.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Summary

  • This is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising tags, or other scripts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Those providers can see your IP address and basic request details.
  • If you book a call, Cal.com collects your booking details. If you email us, ImprovMX forwards the message to our mailbox.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

AZERATE INC (“AZERATE,” “we,” “us”), a Delaware corporation, operates azerateinc.com. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit the site, book a call, or email us.

Visiting the site

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub’s servers receive standard request information, such as your IP address, browser user agent, the page requested, and the time. GitHub may log this for security and to operate the service. We do not receive these logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser requests those files directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address and user agent. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

We don’t set cookies. The site contains no JavaScript and uses no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising tags, session recording, or embedded third-party widgets. We don’t build profiles of visitors.

Booking a call (Cal.com)

Our “Book a fit call” buttons are links to Cal.com, a third-party scheduling service. Information you enter there, such as your name, email address, and any notes, is collected by Cal.com and shared with us so we can schedule and prepare for the call. Cal.com’s handling of your information is governed by the Cal.com Privacy Policy.

Emailing us

Email sent to hello@azerateinc.com is received by ImprovMX, an email-forwarding service, which forwards it to our business mailbox. ImprovMX and our mailbox provider process the message to deliver and store it. We receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message contents.

How we use your information

We use booking details and email correspondence to respond to you, schedule and hold calls, assess whether our services fit your needs, and, if we work together, administer the engagement and keep business records. Data that clients share during a pilot is governed by the signed pilot agreement, not this policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with the service providers described above that help us run the site, scheduling, and email, or when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep emails and booking details only as long as we need them for the purposes above — to respond, follow up on our conversation, and keep reasonable business records — and then delete them. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation. Third-party providers (GitHub, Google, Cal.com, ImprovMX) keep data according to their own policies.

Your requests

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@azerateinc.com. We’ll respond promptly and within the time applicable law requires. We may need to verify your request.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We limit access to our mailbox and booking records to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Children

The site is for businesses and is not directed to children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change how the site works (for example, adding analytics or cookies), we’ll update this policy first. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@azerateinc.com.
