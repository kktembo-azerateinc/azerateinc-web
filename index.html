FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a software subscription?

No. The pilot is a fixed-scope build and handoff. We use your existing tools (or recommend lean ones). Later we may productize — the pilot stands on its own.

What if we already have a VA or SDR?

Good. We automate the repetitive layer so they spend time on conversations that close — not copy-paste triage. Humans stay in the loop where judgment matters.

Is it really 14 days?

It’s a 14-day pilot for one workflow, and it depends on timely access and decisions from your team. Days 1–3 lock the map, and on Day 3 we call go or no-go in writing. Go-live lands around Day 11. SMS follow-up is available after carrier (10DLC) approval and our compliance review, which can take longer — email can go live first.

How does payment work?

50% at kickoff — no work starts until it clears — and 50% Net 7 after acceptance. If we call no-go on Day 3, you get 100% of the kickoff payment back. If you withdraw by Day 3, you get 50% of it back. After Day 3, the kickoff payment is non-refundable.

What do you measure as “proof”?

Median first-response time, % of leads touched by automation, qualification coverage, and booked next-steps. Go-live is around Day 11, so the pilot report covers the live days in the pilot: your baseline vs. after, on your own data. No invented benchmarks, and no promised results.

Who owns the system when you’re done?

You own your accounts, data, configuration, and docs — everything runs in accounts you control. Once the pilot is paid in full, you also get a perpetual license to the AZERATE toolkit embedded in it. We keep our reusable Pilot Kit. You’re not locked in.

Do you record calls?

No. AZERATE never records calls. Any call capture runs only in your own accounts, after you certify your call-consent setup. The default is call-outcome logging. Missed-call text-back is available after carrier (10DLC) approval and our compliance review.

What isn’t included?

Full website redesigns, new paid-media campaigns, custom mobile apps, or open-ended “AI strategy.” Also not included: health data (PHI) or BAAs, and call recording by AZERATE. One workflow, shipped.
