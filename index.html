FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a software subscription?

No. The pilot is a fixed-scope build and handoff. We use your existing tools (or recommend lean ones). Later we may productize — the pilot stands on its own.

What if we already have a VA or SDR?

Good. We automate the repetitive layer so they spend time on conversations that close — not copy-paste triage. Humans stay in the loop where judgment matters.

Can you really ship in 14 days?

Yes — if scope is one workflow and access is fast. Days 1–3 lock the map. If discovery reveals a messier path, we say so before you pay for the build.

What do you measure as “proof”?

Median first-response time, % of leads touched by automation, qualification coverage, and booked next-steps in the pilot window. No invented benchmarks — your baseline vs. after.

Who owns the system when you’re done?

You do. Accounts, automations, and docs stay under your control. We’re available for follow-on work; you’re not locked in.

What isn’t included?

Full website redesigns, new paid-media campaigns, custom mobile apps, or open-ended “AI strategy.” One workflow, shipped.
